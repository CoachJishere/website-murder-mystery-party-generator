### Consumer Trends

- **Vintage vs. modern contrast**: The old-school radio dispatcher contrasted with modern algorithmic dispatch creates a compelling character study in technological displacement and human judgment.

### Consumer Trends

- **Bank robbery scenario**: The classic bank heist is an immediately recognizable and beloved mystery and thriller setting—with the teller as the key witness, accomplice, or victim.
- **Inside job narrative**: A teller with access to vault schedules, cash counts, and security procedures is the ideal inside accomplice—or the person who noticed irregularities that got them killed.
- **Economic vulnerability as motive**: With wages near the bottom of the service sector and career advancement nearly impossible, bank teller characters have credible economic pressure as motivation.
- **Surveillance and control**: Banks are among the most heavily surveilled environments in modern society—CCTV, transaction logs, and security protocols create rich evidence-trail opportunities in mystery scenarios.
- **Financial secrets access**: A bank teller handles transactions daily—they see patterns, notice irregularities, and may witness financial movements that suggest crimes. The teller who saw too much is a compelling victim archetype.
