# Murder Mystery Blog Post Research — Pack 4: Professions & Specialty Venues

## Project Overview

You are conducting research for **mysterymaker.party**, a murder mystery party generator website that creates custom mystery scenarios for various themes, settings, character professions, and venue types.

The website has ~400 English blog posts covering different murder mystery themes, character professions, settings, and event locations. These posts need optimization to rank better in Google search AND to be cited by AI platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.

## Why This Research Matters

Modern content optimization requires a **dual-track approach**:

1. **SEO (Search Engine Optimization)** — Traditional Google ranking using keywords, internal links, meta descriptions
2. **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** — AI platform citations using statistics, expert quotes, and authoritative sources

**Research from Princeton/Georgia Tech shows GEO effectiveness:**
- Statistics with citations: **+30.6% visibility** in AI responses
- Expert quotations: **+40.9% visibility** in AI responses
- Authoritative source citations: **+27% visibility** in AI responses
- Comparison tables: **2.5x citation rate** in AI platforms

Your job is to gather **premium, citation-worthy data** that will make these blog posts rank on Google AND get cited by AI platforms.

## Your Mission

Research **20 character professions and specialty venue types** for murder mystery parties. For each theme, gather:

1. **Market Statistics** (3-5 per theme)
   - Market size, revenue, growth rates
   - Consumer spending patterns
   - Popularity metrics, attendance/participation data
   - Industry trends, demographics

2. **Expert Quotes** (2-3 per theme)
   - Industry experts, authors, psychologists, event planners
   - Full attribution with credentials
   - Must be real, verifiable quotes

3. **Consumer Trends** (3-5 per theme)
   - What people are searching for
   - Emerging preferences
   - Popular variations or sub-niches

## Research Quality Standards

✅ **DO:**

- Cite exact numbers with sources and URLs
- Find recent data (2020-2026 preferred)
- Verify all URLs work

- Use vague language like "many people" or "growing trend" without numbers
- Cite sources without URLs
- Include broken or paywalled links
