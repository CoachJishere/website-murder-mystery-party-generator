### Trends (Zombie Theme)

- **Horror's theatrical market share more than tripled** from 2.69% (2014) to 9.95% (2024), making it the fastest-rising film genre in the US.
- **The Last of Us** became the largest debut-season audience in HBO history (S1) and grew further to ~37 million average viewers per episode in Season 2, with over 90 million total global viewers.
- **The Walking Dead franchise** continues to generate spinoffs that break AMC/AMC+ records — The Ones Who Live drew nearly 3 million premiere viewers (biggest AMC premiere in 6 years).
- **Zombie video games** are massive: Resident Evil 4 Remake sold 10M+ units as the franchise's fastest seller; Dead Island 2 reached 20M+ players.
- **Horror-themed escape rooms grew 28%**, now comprising 33% of new European escape room launches.
- **Gen Z is the #1 horror audience**: 91% watched horror content in 2024, and millennials named it their most-preferred genre (~20% of viewing).
- **Halloween spending surged 32% in five years** (from ~$8B pre-pandemic to $13.1B projected for 2025), with 32% of consumers planning to throw or attend a party.
- **The immersive horror games market** ($8.77B in 2024) is projected to grow at 12.46% CAGR to $22.45B by 2032.

- **Superhero "fatigue" is selective, not universal.** Average global gross dropped from $1.1B (2019) to $453M (2024), but breakout hits like Deadpool & Wolverine ($1.338B) and Superman ($618.7M) prove the genre still delivers blockbusters when execution is strong.

- **X-Men '97 revived animated superhero content**: Disney+'s most-watched animated premiere since What If…?, with the original show's viewership surging 522%.
- **Comic book sales rebounded** to $1.94B in the US/Canada (2024), up 73% from pre-pandemic 2019 levels.
- **Superhero action figures dominate** the $9.7B global market with a 44.6% share.
- **Comic-Con attendance remains at capacity**: SDCC (135K) and NYCC (200K+) were at full capacity in 2024, generating $240M+ combined economic impact.
- **Cosplay is a $5.4B global market** growing at 5.5–7.4% annually, with 18M+ community members.
- **Spider-Man remains the single most popular Halloween costume** for children, with 2.3–2.6 million kids dressing as the character annually.
