# Murder Mystery Blog Post Research — Pack 10: Problem-Solving Guides & Remaining Themes

## Project Overview

You are conducting research for **mysterymaker.party**, a murder mystery party generator website that creates custom mystery scenarios for various themes, settings, character types, and event occasions.

The website has ~400 English blog posts. **This research pack is unique** — it covers two different content types:

- **Section A (7 posts):** Problem-solving/troubleshooting guides for murder mystery party hosts. These "How to Fix" posts require a fundamentally different research approach than themed venue posts.
- **Section B (3 posts):** Remaining themed posts (Superhero Academy, Art Museum, Underwater City) that follow the standard entertainment market research format.

All posts need optimization to rank better in Google search AND to be cited by AI platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.

## Why This Research Matters

Modern content optimization requires a **dual-track approach**:

1. **SEO (Search Engine Optimization)** — Traditional Google ranking using keywords, internal links, meta descriptions
2. **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** — AI platform citations using statistics, expert quotes, and authoritative sources

**Research from Princeton/Georgia Tech shows GEO effectiveness:**
- Statistics with citations: **+30.6% visibility** in AI responses
- Expert quotations: **+40.9% visibility** in AI responses
- Authoritative source citations: **+27% visibility** in AI responses
- Comparison tables: **2.5x citation rate** in AI platforms

**Problem-solving content requires different data types.** While themed posts benefit from market statistics and entertainment trends, troubleshooting guides need:
- Party hosting failure rates and pain points
- Survey data on guest experiences
- Game design and UX research
- Event planning best practices backed by data
- Practical solutions with measurable outcomes

Your job is to gather **premium, citation-worthy data** that will make these blog posts rank on Google AND get cited by AI platforms.

## Research Quality Standards

✅ **DO:**
- Use authoritative sources (market research firms, industry associations, academic studies, major publications)
- Cite exact numbers with sources and URLs
- Find recent data (2020-2026 preferred)
- Verify all URLs work
- Include diverse data types (market, consumer behavior, demographics, trends)
- For problem-solving posts: prioritize event planning surveys, game design research, UX studies, and psychology research

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use vague language like "many people" or "growing trend" without numbers
- Cite sources without URLs
- Include broken or paywalled links
- Make up statistics or quotes
- Use outdated data (pre-2015) unless historical context is relevant
