### Consumer Trends

- **Record Graduation Spending**: The NRF projects a record $6.8 billion in U.S. graduation gift spending for 2025, indicating a growing willingness to invest in celebrating graduates — including experiential celebrations.
- **Gen Z Wants Experiences, Not Stuff**: Over 60% of Gen Z prioritize shareable, tech-forward experiences at celebrations. Murder mystery parties — with their interactive gameplay, photo opportunities, and social sharing potential — align perfectly with this preference.
- **"Gradchella" and Themed Celebrations**: Better Homes & Gardens and other planning resources identify festival-style graduation parties and themed celebrations as trending formats for 2025-2026. Murder mystery themes fit this broader move toward creative, immersive graduation events.
- **DIY Budget Overruns**: Over 40% of DIY graduation party hosts exceed their original budgets by at least 20%, suggesting demand for complete, structured party solutions like murder mystery kits that provide all-in-one entertainment packages.
- **Most Celebrations Are Saturday Events**: About 65% of graduation celebrations fall on Saturdays between late May and mid-July, creating a concentrated demand window for themed party entertainment. Murder mystery parties provide a structured 2-3 hour activity that anchors these celebrations.
