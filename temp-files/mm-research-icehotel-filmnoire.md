## Conclusion: cross-cutting insights for content strategy

Three macro-trends unite all 10 themes. First, **experiential spending is outpacing material consumption** — from wellness retreats to luxury trains to haunted hotels, consumers consistently pay premiums for immersive, story-rich experiences (89% of tourism providers report higher experiential demand). Second, **Gen Z and millennials are reshaping every segment**, driving 41% of U.S. wellness spend, 91% of dark tourism visitation, 50% of PADI certifications, and 36% of European rail tourism. Third, **social media amplification creates outsized market effects** — TikTok's #food (555B views), #Paranormal (5B views), and BookTok's 41.3% boost to sci-fi/fantasy sales demonstrate that digital virality directly translates to tourism and entertainment revenue.

The strongest data availability exists for **wellness/spa** (GWI, ISPA), **beach tourism** (UNWTO, WTTC, CTO), and **detective fiction** (Nielsen, Circana BookScan). The most limited data applies to **ice hotels** (no standalone market reports), **circus arts** (Cirque du Soleil is private; no comprehensive industry statistics), and **underwater hotels** (extremely niche with wildly varying market estimates). For themes with limited primary data, adjacent market statistics — adventure tourism ($464B), luxury travel ($1.59T), dark tourism ($32B) — provide strong contextual anchoring for blog content.
