- **Change (worker `auto-remediate-packages` v28, no SQL).** `detectorSinceIso` in `detector-guard.ts`, applied inside `callDetector`: for exactly two RPCs, `list_packages_with_meta_text_leak` and `list_packages_with_pointform_language_mismatch`, the window is capped at 14 days (from 30). Every other detector keeps its window, the held run's 7 days is already narrower, and a window wider than the standard one (an explicit `backfill_days`) is left alone. The re-detect gates go through the same `callDetector`, so a fix and its check use the same window.
- **Trade-off.** A meta-text leak or pointform language mismatch in a package older than 14 days is no longer auto-healed. Those packages were already seen by the new-purchase sweep, the reviewer-before-release pipeline and the 14 days of runs before it (48 runs a day), so the lost coverage is for something that survived all of that. An old package with such a leak found by hand is repaired by hand or with `backfill_days`.
- **Expected effect, not yet measured.** Packages scanned fall from 57 to roughly 30 for these two (about half the work). The honest limit: this halves the load, it does not remove the contention that makes a 2.2 s query exceed 8 s. If skips continue, the next steps in order are: a `pg_stat_activity` snapshot during a full run, then staggering the cron minutes (ten jobs fire at :00/:10/:50), then a stored per-package verdict so unchanged packages are not rescanned.
