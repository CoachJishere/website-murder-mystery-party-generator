## What is Night of Mystery?

Night of Mystery is a well-established maker of downloadable murder mystery party games. You browse their catalog of pre-written themes — 1920s speakeasies, Hollywood galas, masquerade balls — pick one that fits your event, and buy the kit sized for your expected number of guests. The scripts are professionally written, the themes are polished, and thousands of hosts have run their parties on them. It's a proven, dependable option.

## What is a custom AI murder mystery kit?

A custom AI murder mystery kit takes a different approach. Instead of choosing from a fixed catalog, you describe the party you want — the theme, the tone, the number of guests, even ages and reading levels — and the generator writes an original mystery around it. With [Mystery Maker's generator](/mystery/create), you answer a few questions in a guided chat and get a complete, ready-to-host package: character sheets, host guide, clues, and printable evidence, all built for your specific event and delivered in minutes.

## Pricing

**Night of Mystery** typically prices kits per theme, and often per guest-count tier — a game sized for 8 guests costs less than the same game sized for 20. If your group grows, you may need to buy up to a larger version. This is straightforward and predictable: you know exactly what you're getting before you buy.

**A custom AI kit** is a single flat price for the whole game, regardless of how many characters you need. Because the content is generated rather than pre-packaged, scaling up your guest list doesn't change the cost. For larger or still-unconfirmed groups, the flat-rate model tends to work out more economical.

**Bottom line:** For a small, fixed group, both land in a similar range. For larger parties, or if your headcount is still shifting, the flat-price custom kit is easier to budget.

## Replayability

**Night of Mystery** games are written to be run once as-is. You can absolutely host the same theme again for a different group of friends, but the plot, culprit, and clues stay the same — so it loses its magic with anyone who's already played it.

**A custom AI kit** is effectively unlimited. Want a 1920s mystery in the spring and a haunted-manor one at Halloween? Generate a brand-new scenario each time. Even within the same theme, you can create a fresh plot with a different culprit, so your regular game-night crew never sees the same solution twice.

**Bottom line:** If you host mystery nights more than once, the generator's ability to produce endless new scenarios is its biggest long-term advantage.

## Guest-count flexibility

**Night of Mystery** kits are built for specific guest-count ranges. That works beautifully when your numbers match a kit exactly — but real parties are messy. A last-minute cancellation or two surprise plus-ones can leave you juggling spare roles or short a character.

**A custom AI kit** is generated around your actual guest list. Need a game for 11 people? You get exactly 11 characters. Mystery Maker also makes it easier to adapt when someone drops out, so a no-show doesn't unravel the plot.

**Bottom line:** If your guest count is exact and stable, a pre-sized kit is fine. If it's uncertain — as most parties are — the custom option removes the guesswork.

## Printability

**Night of Mystery** delivers print-ready PDFs, and the formatting is polished and designed to look great on paper — a real strength if presentation matters to you.

**A custom AI kit** is also built to print at home. With Mystery Maker you get clean, print-friendly character sheets, host guide, clues, and evidence cards you can run off on a home printer — nothing to wait for, and nothing to re-buy if you reprint for a bigger group.

**Bottom line:** Both print well. The difference is timing and cost — a generated kit is instant and reprintable at no extra charge.
